How It Works

Three changes.
Full visibility.

01
A label goes on every production unit

When an order starts, you print a small QR label and attach it to the unit moving through production — a tray, a batch carrier, the part itself. Takes seconds. Replaces whatever you're using now: paper slips, chalk marks, memory.

02
Cameras read the labels automatically

At each station in your workflow, a mounted camera reads the QR label as the unit passes and records the time. Workers do nothing differently — no scanning, no buttons, no apps to learn.

03
You see everything on one screen

A live dashboard shows every active order, its current stage, how long it has been there, and which orders are running behind. Accessible from any device on your local network.
